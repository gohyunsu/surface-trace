HOW IT WORKS

From photo to highlight.

01

Build the reference

Normal photos become feature maps; sampled patches form the reference.

02

Prepare the photo

Resize, convert to grayscale, and normalize brightness.

03

Split into patches

Examine small areas across the surface.

04

Measure features

Capture brightness, dark gaps, and edges.

05

Find the nearest normal patch

Score each patch by its closest normal match.

06

Map anomalies

Map patch scores and flag photos above the threshold.
